Supported Agronomic Profiles:

  • Rice (Paddy): Sowing (0-15d) → Vegetative (15-60d) → Reproductive (60-90d) → Harvest (90-120d)
  • Wheat: Germination (0-10d) → Tillering (10-40d) → Heading (40-90d) → Ripening (90-120d)
  • Cotton: Planting (0-15d) → Squaring (15-45d) → Blooming (45-100d) → Defoliation (100-150d)
