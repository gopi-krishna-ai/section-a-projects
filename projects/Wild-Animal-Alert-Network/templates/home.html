🐾 Wild Animal Alert Network

AI-powered intrusion detection and deterrent system for human-wildlife conflict resolution.

Discover Smart Conservation

The Wild Animal Alert Network is an intelligent monitoring system designed to detect and classify animals entering restricted or populated areas. Utilizing advanced YOLO-based object detection, the system can instantly identify various species, determine their dietary type (Carnivorous, Omnivorous, Herbivorous), and classify them as a potential threat (Wild) or safe.

Upon detecting a dangerous wild animal, the system triggers real-time alarms and deploys deterrents, ensuring the safety of local communities and crops while minimizing harm to the wildlife.

🔍 Real-Time Detection

Fast and accurate identification of animals using state-of-the-art AI models, processing feeds instantly to categorize the species.

📊 Diet & Classification

Not just detection—our system provides detailed insights about the animal, including its name and diet type.

🚨 Automated Alerts

If a wild animal (like a bear, elephant, or zebra) is detected, an immediate alarm is triggered to alert authorities and locals.

🛡️ Safe Area Monitoring

Domestic or safe animals are tracked without triggering panic alarms, providing a clear summary of the area's safety status.
