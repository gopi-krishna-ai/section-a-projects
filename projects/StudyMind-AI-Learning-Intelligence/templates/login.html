How It Works

StudyMind is an advanced AI-powered learning workspace that transforms any PDF, scanned notes, or text document into a complete study toolkit. Here's your step-by-step guide:

1. Upload Documents

Upload PDFs, images of handwritten notes, or text files. The system extracts text using PyMuPDF, with OCR fallback for scanned pages.

2. AI Understanding

Your document is chunked into passages and encoded into semantic embeddings using the MiniLM-L6 sentence transformer model.

3. Ask Questions

Ask any question about your documents. The DistilBERT-SQuAD model finds precise answers with confidence scores and source pages.

4. Auto Flashcards & Quizzes

AI automatically generates flashcards and fill-in-the-blank quizzes from key concepts extracted from your documents.

5. Smart Summaries

Get both extractive (key sentences) and abstractive (AI-rewritten) summaries of your entire document in seconds.

6. Study Plan

The system generates a structured study plan with topic organization, importance ranking, and a day-by-day revision schedule.
