Intelligent Disaster Relief Management

ResQAI leverages advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (Support Vector Machines) to automatically detect, classify, and verify emergency situations from social media streams in real-time.

Access Command Center

Step 1: Data Ingestion

The system pulls live social media data and parses it. As a user, you can also manually input disaster reports into the AI Analyzer for instant classification.

Step 2: AI Classification

Our SVM model evaluates the text using TF-IDF mathematical weights, distinguishing between genuine emergencies and figurative speech (e.g., "movie was a disaster").

Step 3: Web Verification

The Web Assistant cross-references the flagged disaster against live global news databases, returning structured intelligence and verifiable proof URLs.
