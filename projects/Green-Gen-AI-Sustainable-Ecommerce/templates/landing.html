The Problem & The Solution

Why Green Gen AI?

The growing demand for sustainable shopping has highlighted the limitations of conventional e-commerce platforms, which rarely provide clear environmental information or recycling incentives. We assign an Eco Score to every product, enabling you to identify environmentally responsible options easily.

How Our Recommendation Engine Works

  1. We log user interactions like viewing products, adding to the cart, and purchasing.
  2. Our Machine Learning model pulls product data (category, material, process) and computes a TF-IDF matrix.
  3. We calculate the Cosine Similarity between all products in the catalog.
  4. The system identifies your favorite categories and average preferred Eco Score.
  5. It ranks similar products and generates a personalized list of greener recommendations, explaining exactly why they were chosen.

Core Features

Transparent Eco Score

Every product is evaluated on a 0-100 scale using metrics like material sustainability, recyclability, carbon footprint, and packaging type.

Explainable AI

The AI doesn't just list products; it explains why a product is recommended (e.g., "Greener alternative to your typical choices").

Recycle & Earn

Submit requests to return used products. Once verified, earn "Green Points" to encourage a circular economy.

3D Customization

Real-time 3D product visualization, allowing you to modify colors and materials directly in the browser before purchasing.

Sustainability Dashboard

Track your environmental impact, average order Eco Score, and recycling contributions over time directly from your profile.

Offline & Self-Contained

Powered by local ML models and a predefined dataset of 1,500 products, ensuring complete privacy and speed.
