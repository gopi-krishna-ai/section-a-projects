Bulk NLP Parsing
Copy-paste entire comment sections from Amazon or Yelp. Our system instantly tokenizes text, removes stop-words, and lemmatizes thousands of reviews at once.

TF-IDF Vectorization
We convert raw review text into numerical feature vectors using Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency, mapping the true semantic intent behind the words.
🤖
Logistic Regression
Trained on massive Kaggle datasets, our ML classifier instantly categorizes reviews as Genuine or Fake based on statistical patterns invisible to human moderators.
🔍
Explainable AI (XAI)
Don't just get a "Fake" label. The dashboard highlights the exact deceptive trigger words that caused the AI to flag the comment, providing complete transparency.
